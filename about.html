About LAMBERT

Functional hardware, built for the people who install it.

LAMBERT is a functional-hardware brand for cabinet makers, kitchen studios, and wardrobe installers. We focus on the parts that move — pull-out baskets, dish drawers, corner systems, trouser racks, and rotating shoe carousels — engineered to fit standard cabinet dimensions and built to survive daily use.

Factory-direct supply
